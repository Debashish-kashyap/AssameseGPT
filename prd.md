# Product Overview

AssameseGPT is a ChatGPT-style conversational AI platform powered by custom Assamese language models.

The platform enables users to interact with AssameseGPT through a modern chat interface supporting Assamese, English, and multilingual conversations.

The long-term vision is to become the primary AI assistant for Assamese speakers while also supporting enterprise and educational use cases.

---

# Product Vision

Build the most capable AI assistant for Assamese language users while maintaining a world-class conversational experience comparable to ChatGPT and Claude.
